Overview

A month of building, not just watching.

The Web Developer Internship Program is a one-month remote programme run by Primeor Solutions, a data analytics and consulting company. Interns work through three levels of front-end tasks, from styled HTML pages to responsive layouts and a complete landing page, and finish with a portfolio of work they can show.

Learn More

Questions, answered.

Do I have to complete every level?

Interns can choose tasks from Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3. Completing more advanced tasks makes your portfolio stronger.

How do I submit my work?

Upload your projects to GitHub, and host them on Netlify or Vercel if possible. Keep a separate folder for each level and submit everything together as one ZIP file using the link shared by the HR team.

Is the internship remote?

Yes. The programme runs online, so you can take part from anywhere.

What do I get at the end?

A completion certificate from Primeor Solutions and a set of projects you can show in your resume and on LinkedIn.
